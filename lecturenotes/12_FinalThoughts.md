## Current Problems in Computing

- Moore's Law is almost dead
    - Transistors are getting smaller, but at some point will hit physical limits
    - Power consumption is a big problem
    - Heat is a bigger problem
    - Busses with parallel wires have a problem with electromagnetic interference (EMI) and crosstalk

### Optical Computing

- Use light instead of electricity
- Faster
- More bandwidth
- Less power consumption
- Less heat (lower losses than resistance)
- Needed: photonics device equivalent of transistors

### Quantum Computing

- In digital circuits, each bit is either 0 or 1
- In quantum circuits, each qbit has a superposition of 0 and 1
- By building a circuit with constraints, the qbits can be made to collapse to a solution
- Quantum computers can theoretically solve certain problems much faster than classical computers
  - Biggest problem: decoherence (qbits decay fast)


### DNA Computing

- Data storage is growing exponentially
- All current methods do not have long enough lifetimes
- DNA is one very promising approach
  - Chemically stable
  - Can store vast amounts of information
- DNA Letters: A, C, G, T
